# Training materials review

Scope: `docs/training/Warehouse-Wizard-User-Manual.md`, `docs/training/Warehouse-Wizard-Training-Facilitator-Guide.md`, and the generated decks/PDFs in `docs/training/artifacts/`. Checked against the live app's routes (`src/App.tsx`), the in-app Help Center source (`src/lib/help-content.ts`), and `docs/admin-guide.md`, which the manual itself names as a source.

## What's already solid

- Product version (1.27), role list, and all 22 routes in `src/App.tsx` line up with what the manual documents — no orphaned or missing modules.
- Every "Parallel Help ID" cited in the manual matches a real `id` in `help-content.ts`.
- `training-materials-manifest.json` matches the actual files on disk — nothing listed is missing, nothing present is unlisted.
- The workflow/exception structure (stop-rule, whole-pallet picking, offline recovery) is consistent across the manual, the facilitator guide, and `help-content.ts`.

## Gaps found

1. **NetSuite integration is undocumented.** `help-content.ts` has a full `netsuite-integration` Help article, and `docs/admin-guide.md` calls it out as "the first integration connection" admins configure. Neither the User Manual, the Facilitator Guide, nor either slide deck mentions it. Any client with ERP sync questions has nothing to point to.
2. **Zebra/ZPL detail is thin.** The manual's Section 15 has one line ("use the configured Zebra/ZPL path"). The actual Help article (`zebra-printing`) covers printer stations, template/print-job audit trail, Code 128 vs GS1-128, and SSCC — none of that made it into the manual.
3. **Warehouse Brain is named but not explained.** Sections 5 and 24.1 mention "management recommendations" but never describe the accept/dismiss/resolve-with-reason workflow that `help-content.ts` documents, or what signals it watches (holds, quarantine, expiry, variance, dock status).
4. **Trusted badge sign-in is under-specified.** The manual says badge/PIN "may be available after a normal password sign-in." It omits the 30-day trust expiry and that badge sign-in is disabled on desktop — both operationally relevant for supervisors setting expectations on shared devices.
5. **Critical RF disconnect acknowledgment is incomplete.** The manual says the alert "requires acknowledgment"; it doesn't mention the *typed challenge* required to dismiss the toast, or that it's shown to supervisors, managers, admins, and developers specifically.
6. **Two glossary terms are used but never explained in body text.** DPMO appears in the Office dashboard (per `help-content.ts`) and SSCC appears in the Zebra Help article, but the manual's own glossary either omits SSCC entirely or lists a term without ever using it in the flow text where a trainee would encounter it.
7. **The facilitator guide has no track for IT/integration stakeholders.** The audience line lists "key client stakeholders" generically, but the standard 60-minute session has no slot or question list for the person who owns ERP or printer infrastructure decisions — which matters once NetSuite and Zebra content exists.

## What I generated

Rather than edit your approved, versioned manual in place, I made enhanced copies in `docs/training/claude/`:

- `Warehouse-Wizard-User-Manual.md` — your original content, unchanged, plus a new addendum section (Section 26) covering the six gaps above: NetSuite integration overview, expanded Zebra/ZPL and label-standards detail, Warehouse Brain workflow, trusted badge sign-in specifics, the RF-disconnect typed-challenge detail, and the missing glossary terms.
- `Warehouse-Wizard-Training-Facilitator-Guide.md` — your original content, plus an optional 10–15 minute IT/Admin add-on module (for sessions where an integration or infrastructure owner is in the room) and three new client questions covering ERP sync and printer-station ownership.

These are drafts for your review — not a replacement for the controlled WW-UM-001 revision. If you want them folded into the official manual, that requires a new revision number and owner/approver sign-off per the manual's own front matter.
